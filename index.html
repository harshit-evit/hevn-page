About HEVN

Who We Are

Himachal EV Network (HEVN) is a dedicated electric mobility infrastructure platform focused on building and operating EV charging stations across the state of Himachal Pradesh.

The initiative aligns with the vision for electric mobility adoption and sustainable transportation ecosystems.

Vision

To build the Himalayan region's most reliable and sustainable electric mobility ecosystem, enabling clean transportation across mountains while preserving the environment and empowering local communities.

Mission

“Advancing Himalayan Electric Mobility through Research-Driven Infrastructure and Intelligent Charging Networks.”

At HEVN, our mission is to develop a resilient and future-ready EV charging ecosystem across Himachal Pradesh by combining research, innovative infrastructure, and smart energy management systems.

We aim to enable seamless EV mobility across the Himalayan terrain while supporting tourism, sustainability goals, and the transition toward clean transportation.

Our Mission Pillars

  • Statewide EV Charging Network: Develop strategically planned infrastructure across H.P. for reliable charging on highways, towns, and remote regions.
  • Tourism-Friendly EV Travel Corridors: Enable clean travel along major tourism routes via green corridors.
  • Sustainability & Climate Commitment: Promote eco-responsible mobility aligned with clean energy transitions.
  • Intelligent & Reliable Charging: Deliver smart charging with advanced monitoring and reliable energy systems.

The Project

Himachal EV Charging Network

HEVN is implementing a strategic EV charging infrastructure project across Himachal Pradesh under a Public Private Partnership (PPP) framework.

The project includes deployment of AC and DC charging stations at key transit locations including Major Green Corridors and Tourism Routes.

Strategic Locations

Sites selected to ensure optimal coverage across the mountainous terrain, including transit corridors and tourist hotspots.

Charging Infrastructure

Deployment of both AC and DC Charging capabilities with renewable energy integration and wayside amenities.

Smart Platform

Supported by a smart digital platform featuring 24x7 network monitoring, mobile app integration, and seamless digital payments.

Why the Himalayas Need a Specialized EV Network

The Himalayan region presents unique geographical, climatic, and infrastructure challenges that require a specialized approach to electric mobility infrastructure. Unlike plains or urban environments, EV charging networks in mountainous regions must be designed with careful consideration of terrain conditions, power availability, travel distances, and tourism mobility patterns.

At HEVN, our infrastructure strategy is built around research and real-world operational insights from the Himalayan region.

Key Challenges in Himalayan EV Infrastructure

⛰️ Mountain Terrain & Elevation

Steep gradients and high-altitude locations impact vehicle energy consumption and charging demand patterns.

🛤️ Sparse Infrastructure

Long travel distances between towns require strategically placed charging stations to ensure uninterrupted EV travel.

⚡ Power Availability

Remote locations often require smart energy management and grid optimization for reliable charging operations.

🚗 Tourism Mobility

Seasonal tourist inflow creates fluctuating charging demand along key travel corridors.

HEVN's Research-Driven Approach

To address these challenges, HEVN is developing a corridor-based EV charging network supported by infrastructure planning, intelligent technology systems, and sustainable energy integration.

⚡ Strategic Corridor Planning

Charging stations placed at optimal intervals along major travel routes.

📊 Data-Driven Design

Using mobility patterns, terrain research, and EV adoption trends to plan deployment.

🔌 Reliable Technology

Deployment of AC and DC charging systems designed in-house for mountain conditions with smart compact substations.

🌱 Sustainable Ecosystem

Supporting environmentally responsible travel while strengthening regional tourism infrastructure.

Building the Backbone of Himalayan Electric Mobility

Through the development of EV corridors and smart charging hubs, HEVN aims to create a reliable electric mobility network across Himachal Pradesh, enabling travelers, residents, and corporates to adopt clean transportation with confidence.
