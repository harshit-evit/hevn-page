About HEVN

Who We Are

Himachal EV Network (HEVN) is a dedicated electric mobility infrastructure platform focused on building and operating EV charging stations across the state of Himachal Pradesh.

The initiative aligns with the Government of India’s vision for electric mobility adoption and sustainable transportation ecosystems.

Vision

To establish Himachal Pradesh as a fully EV-enabled hill state, enabling reliable charging infrastructure across highways, tourist destinations, and urban centres.

Mission

  • Develop a statewide EV charging network
  • Enable tourism-friendly EV travel corridors
  • Support government sustainability initiatives
  • Provide reliable and intelligent charging services

Promoted By

HEVN is proudly promoted by EVI Technologies (Parent Company), a technology-driven company with extensive experience in energy systems, infrastructure deployment, and smart mobility solutions.

The Project

Himachal EV Charging Network

HEVN is implementing a strategic EV charging infrastructure project across Himachal Pradesh under a Public Private Partnership (PPP) framework.

The project includes deployment of AC and DC fast charging stations at key government locations including Major Transit Green Corridors and Tourism Routes.

Strategic Locations

Sites selected to ensure optimal coverage across the mountainous terrain, including transit corridors and tourist hotspots.

Charging Infrastructure

Deployment of both AC and DC Fast Charging capabilities with renewable energy integration and wayside amenities.

Smart Platform

Supported by a smart digital platform featuring 24x7 network monitoring, mobile app integration, and seamless digital payments.

Sustainability Impact

HEVN is committed to building infrastructure that supports India’s Net Zero goals and reduces carbon emissions from transportation.

By enabling EV adoption in a hill state like Himachal Pradesh, HEVN contributes to sustainable tourism and cleaner mountain ecosystems.
